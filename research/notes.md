# LYNN — research notes

Researched 16 October 2026. Everything below comes from LYNN's own channels (Instagram, owner posts and owner photos on Google), its Google listing, and named third-party sources. Nothing here is guessed unless it says so.

## Identity

- **Name.** Their captions, Google listing and menus all say **LYNN** in capitals ("LYNN offers…", "LYNN's Christmas menu", "SUNDAY ROAST At LYNN"). The menu header reads "LYNN RESTAURANT". The fascia over the door has "Lynn" hand-brushed in black script with a trailing tail.
- **Logo.** The Instagram avatar (and the foot of the drinks menu) is a very condensed, tall **LYNN** wordmark. The letters lock together into one block: the L's foot runs under the Y's stem, and the two Ns share a double-width middle stem. On the avatar it's white on black; on the drinks menu it's grey with a thin dark bevel. Traced to `logo/lynn-wordmark-traced.svg`, within about a pixel of the menu artwork.
- **Address.** 14 St Martins Square, Leicester LE1 5DF (Google lists it as "Floor 0 · St Martin's Square"). Plus code JVP7+2V.
- **Phone.** +44 7342 375899 (Google listing, which the owner maintains). Be-Lavie also prints 0116 319 1390.
- **Instagram.** @lynn_leics. Display name "Lynn", 4,813 followers, 33 posts. Location tag on posts: "St Martin's Square".
- **Website.** None. The Google listing literally shows "Add website".
- **Booking.** Google "Reserve a table", run through **SevenRooms**. Tonight (Fri 16 Oct) it offered slots from 6:00 to 8:00 PM. Captions say "Book your table click the link in our bio".
- **Not to confuse with.** Lyn's Cafe (205 Narborough Road, LE3), St Martin's Coffee Shop / Crafty (2–6 St Martins Walk), Kai (2–4 St Martins Square), Restaurant 263 (76 Highcross St). None of their material is used.
- **Still trading?** Yes. Owner post on Google two hours before this research ("Our 2026 XMAS Menu is here!"). Instagram posts on 15, 8, 4 and 2 Oct and 28 and 24 Sep 2026. Be-Lavie guide updated 10 Sep 2026.
- **Background (third party).** Be-Lavie: "From the chefs that brought Leicester the former 2 Rosette Restaurant 263, comes Lynn Restaurant." Visit Leicester: "Lynn is a café by day and Bistro by night, with small and large plates inspired by cuisine from around the world." Visit Leicester (April 2026) and Love Business East Midlands (May 2026) list it among the city's new openings.

## The room (from owner and customer photos)

- **Shopfront.** Full-height glazing in black frames under a white fascia with the brushed "Lynn" script. Red-brick piers either side. An "In. Independent Leicester" sticker and the food-hygiene sticker on the glass.
- **Window lettering.** Vinyl stacked in a thin geometric sans: **COFFEE / BRUNCH / COCKTAILS / DINNER**.
- **Outside.** Hairpin-leg plank tables and dark metal chairs (2025). Black wicker bistro chairs on aluminium frames round a dimpled steel table (2026). A wooden A-board chalked in white and yellow.
- **Inside.**
  - Exposed red brick, a white-painted ceiling with the ducts showing, and clusters of clear glass globe pendants with filament bulbs.
  - Black enamel pendants over a white metro-tiled counter with a dark top. Blush-pink plaster walls with dark-framed chalkboards.
  - Wooden shelves of wine bottles, and a long window bar with stools.
  - An open kitchen with a speckled granite pass. A reviewer: "you can see the chefs prepare your food, they also bring it over to you".
- **On the tables.**
  - Dark rough-sawn oak boards. Pampas grass in white ceramic vases, and tealights in ribbed glass.
  - Lumpy celadon-green glazed vases holding gypsophila and leaves.
  - Speckled sand stoneware plates, oatmeal cross-hatched bowls, and white plates with a bronze rim.
  - Mustard-yellow and grey cups and saucers.
- **Light.** By day, soft and pale: blush walls, white tile. By night, warm and amber: the globes glowing behind the black window frame. The owner's own shopfront photo is the night view.

## What's weak or missing online

1. **No website.** The menus live as images: a drinks PNG on Google, a Christmas menu on Instagram. There's no brunch menu or dinner menu with prices anywhere online.
2. **People don't know what LYNN does.** A Google reviewer says it outright: "how about putting a menu on the windows so others can see what you do as they pass?"
3. **The day/night split gets lost.** Listings call it a café (Google, Urbanary), "Lynn Restaurant" (Be-Lavie), or "café by day and Bistro by night" (Visit Leicester). The evening offer (dinner, cocktails, Sunday roast) is the part that needs booking, and it's the least visible.
4. **Hours are inconsistent across third-party listings.** Urbanary drops Wednesday.
5. **Booking is buried.** It sits behind "link in bio" or the Google reserve button.
6. **Name clash in search.** Searches for "Lynn Leicester" return Lyn's Cafe (Narborough Road) and King's Lynn first. Search engines barely index @lynn_leics.
7. **Thin footprint.** 33 Instagram posts, 36 Google reviews, no TripAdvisor listing found, no delivery apps.
8. **Two marks in use.** The script on the fascia and the condensed wordmark online.
9. For the owner's awareness, not the site: the FSA food hygiene rating is 3 (inspected 17 Apr 2026).

## Design reasoning

- **Palette** comes from the room and the logo.
  - Ink #12110E is the black of the window frames and the avatar.
  - Plaster #EAD6D0 is the blush wall. Tile #F5F1EA is the metro tiles. Oak #5E3A1E is the tables. Stoneware #B98E62 is the speckled plates. Glaze #7F8C5A is the green vases. Mustard #D9961E is the cups. Brick #A2543B.
  - At night the page turns to ink, with filament amber #F2B15C, which is the globe pendants in the owner's own shopfront photo.
- **Type.**
  - The traced LYNN mark is the logo.
  - Headings are set in Antonio, a tall condensed grotesque, so they sit with the mark.
  - The four window words are set in Josefin Sans, the closest open font to the thin geometric vinyl on the glass.
  - Body text is Fira Sans, a humanist sans close to the drinks menu's face, and it holds up at phone sizes.
- **Layout.** The page is built like their shopfront. The first screen is the owner's night photo cut by black window bars, with the window words on the glass. After that it's a run of panels that follow the day: counter, brunch, evening, Sunday, Christmas, visit. There's no hero banner, no card grid and no testimonial carousel. The review lines are short credited quotes placed next to the thing they praise.
- **Signature interaction 1: the window.** COFFEE / BRUNCH / COCKTAILS / DINNER light up according to the real Leicester time and LYNN's hours. Dragging the clock along the bottom of the window runs the day forward: the words switch on and off, and as evening arrives the whole site dims to ink and the globe pendants glow. That's "café by day, bistro by night", told with their own window, and it answers the reviewer who wanted to see "what you do as they pass".
- **Signature interaction 2: the counter.** The owner's cannoli-cabinet photo with its five real flavour cards. Tap a card and the photo glides to that tray. It's the thing Be-Lavie singles out, shown the way it looks in the shop.
- **Phone first.** There's a sticky bottom bar with Call, Directions and Book. Hours show "open now / closes at" for today. Menus are readable text rather than images.

## What the owner would most want this website to do

1. Fill the evenings and Sundays: make Friday and Saturday dinner, the Sunday roast and the Christmas menu as easy to find as brunch, with booking one tap away.
2. Put the menu in the window: show anyone on a phone in or near St Martins Square what LYNN serves right now, and what it costs, before they walk in.
3. Give word of mouth an address: one findable home for LYNN, not Lyn's and not the coffee shop next door, with the right hours, phone and directions.
